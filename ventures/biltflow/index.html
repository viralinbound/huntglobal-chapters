Construction Intelligence

Construction Intelligence. Built to think ahead.

Biltflow is one AI-native system for running your entire construction operation — from development and design to procurement, commercial, delivery and asset care. Trained on data from more than $5 billion in live construction projects, Biltflow brings proven construction intelligence to every workflow.

The problem

Disconnected data creates uncertainty.

Drawings, costs, contracts and site records live in separate systems that don’t talk to each other. Teams waste time chasing information, decisions get delayed, and critical insights are lost — and what one building teaches rarely reaches the next.

How Biltflow works

Sees it. Solves it. Does it.

Biltflow sees what others miss, works out what happens next, and gets the work done. You decide how far it goes — Manual, Copilot or Autopilot.

Manual

You’re in control.

Biltflow gives you the intelligence to act.

Copilot

Work together.

Biltflow does the work. You review and approve.

Autopilot

Approved workflows happen automatically, within the rules you set.
