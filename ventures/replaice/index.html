Construction & compliance OS

BiltFlow

The intelligent operating system for construction.

Connect feasibility, drawings and takeoff with procurement, project delivery, claims and reporting.

Explore BiltFlow

One connected construction lifecycle.

  1. SiteScope — Assess site feasibility and carry project constraints and assumptions into planning.
  2. Pulse — Build the programme, connecting phases, dependencies and milestones to delivery dates.
  3. Vault — Manage drawings, documents and revisions that define the scope for estimating and procurement.
  4. BOQ Engine & Estimator — Turn reviewed drawings into quantities and cost estimates for trade packages.
  5. ProcureFlow — Take those packages through tendering, bid comparison and contract award, aligned with programme requirements.
  6. PayFlow — Assess progress claims against awarded scope, contract terms and milestones, then certify approved amounts for payment.
  7. ResourceFlow — Use programme dates to forecast workforce demand and coordinate allocations across projects throughout delivery.
