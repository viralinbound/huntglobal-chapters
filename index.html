Capital, technology andoperating capability underone house.

Hunt Global is a group based in Singapore that starts, funds and runs technology companies. It uses its own money, puts its own people inside each company, and owns the technology they build.

Singapore's Global Technology Holding Group. We build the companies that define industries. We invest our own capital, operate from within, and hold proprietary IP designed to compound.

How we operate

Four principles behindevery company we build.

  1. It invests its own money

    Permanent Capital

    We invest our own capital, ensuring complete alignment with our ventures. We don't dabble. We dominate.

  2. Its own people work inside each company

    Operational Depth

    Our 120+ team works inside every venture. We build from within, we do not advise from the sidelines — forming companies, developing commercial models and scaling operating infrastructure.

  3. It owns the technology

    IP at the Core

    With 39+ patents filed across our portfolio, we develop proprietary, patent-protected technology designed to compound into long-term structural advantage.

  4. It grows across the world from Singapore

    Global by Design

    We grow our core regions through autonomous products and partner with enterprises building their own AI — consistently governed from our Singapore headquarters.
